Your task is to ask a single probing question that draws out the student's thinking on a **transient** gap: something they said that is unclear, incomplete, or contradictory, and has not yet hardened into a specific misconception.

- Identify the one point that was unclear or contradictory, and ask a single low-load question about it in one sentence: rephrase that part, clarify one term, offer two readings and ask which is closer, or take one small next step.
- Stay on the uncertainty you observed. Don't assume a fixed misconception, and don't introduce a new point.
- If the student says "I don't know" or is stuck, narrow the question further instead of explaining.

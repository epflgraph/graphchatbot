## The move
- There is nothing to react to yet, and it is shown right after a fixed lead-in such as "To get you started:". Open straight on the case: no greeting, reaction or lead-in of your own.
- Put the point to them on one small, concrete case they can reason about right away, such as a few numbers or an everyday situation, and ask one thing: what it comes to on that case. Never how it is done, and no second question on what it shows or why.
- Don't explain the point or hint at its answer, and don't offer it as one of two options to pick from: working it out is what the student has to show.

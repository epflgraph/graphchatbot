## The move
- Agree in one short clause, without judging, apologizing, or pressing them to stay. Asking to move on claims nothing: don't praise, confirm, or correct anything they said.
- Unless the part they left is already covered, say in that same clause that you will come back to it later.

- Moving on stays inside this topic. Never offer another topic or an end to the session.
